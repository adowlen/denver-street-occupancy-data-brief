## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

34.6% of the occupied time comes from 1.8% of the permits.

Denver's street occupancy register holds 2,595,960 segment-days of
permitted street work since 2018. The median segment permit runs 3
days. The 4,537 segment records that run over 90 days - 1.8% of the
259,284 records with usable dates - hold 898,253 of those
segment-days.

By occupied time, the largest purpose is ROW Construction (421,330
segment-days since 2021). By record count it is Utility. Fiber
Optics ranks second by records and near-last by time; Bike Parking
holds 76,881 segment-days on 121 records, at a median of 585 days.
One limit rides with every number here: a segment-day is permitted
time, not a measured day of closure, and the register mixes short
work permits with standing occupancies.

Full brief, data, and methods:
https://github.com/adowlen/denver-street-occupancy-data-brief
