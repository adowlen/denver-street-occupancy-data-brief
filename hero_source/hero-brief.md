## Scene description (story-driven, not decorative)

A dusk Denver street seen from a slightly elevated angle: one long
work closure stretches down the street into the distance - amber
barriers, an open trench with a water-main pipe section, a trencher
crew at work, teal fiber-optic cable spools, traffic narrowed by cones
to one lane - and a fenced foreground segment with a weathered
dumpster marking an occupation that has stood a long time, while
shorter closures come and go further along. The visual argument is
the brief's: occupied time is concentrated in a small number of long
occupations, not spread evenly across permits. Brand palette: dark
charcoal base, amber and teal accents. Clean dark field across the
top third for the headline; no text baked into the art.
